Hermes Personal

Hermes Personal is a private automation tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account (Gmail and related services).

Privacy Policy

  • The app is not offered to the public. The only user is the owner.
  • It accesses only the owner's own Google data, on the owner's own computer.
  • No Google user data is collected from anyone else, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of Service

This tool is for the owner's personal use only and is provided with no warranty. It is not available to any other users.
